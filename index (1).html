Undergraduate Engineering & IT Programs

Where the coastline meets the circuit board.

ECOAST trains the people who will build, wire, code and design what comes next — six bachelor's programs spanning software, structures and systems, taught by faculty who still work in the field.

Built for people who'd rather build than watch.

Every ECOAST program pairs classroom fundamentals with hands-on work — in a lab, on a site, or in a codebase — from the first year.

Faculty from the field

Instructors who currently practice — site engineers, working developers, licensed electricians — teaching what they use, not just what's printed.

Real labs, real tools

Structural, circuit, and software labs stocked for actual project work — not demonstrations behind glass.

Programs that cross over

Shared first-year coursework across engineering and IT tracks, so switching lanes early doesn't cost you a year.

Six programs, one campus.

Each is a full bachelor's degree. Codes below match the ones you'll see on your transcript and ID.
